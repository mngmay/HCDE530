# Research Bridge

Research Bridge helps UX researchers and designers turn raw research into audience-ready insights. You add your research data and notes, and the tool writes insight summaries tailored to different audiences. This cuts down on manual work, speeds up the research process, and makes it easier to share findings at scale.

## Who It's For

Researchers, designers, product managers, and anyone who needs to share data, insights, and plans with a specific audience.
