# Research Bridge

Research Bridge is a tool that helps user experience researchers and designers turn raw research into audience-ready insights. Users input research data and notes, and the tool generates insight summaries tailored to different audiences—reducing manual effort, speeding up the research process, and making it possible to share findings at scale.

## Who It's For

Researchers, designers, product managers, and anyone who needs to tailor the communication of data, insights, and plans to a specific audience.
